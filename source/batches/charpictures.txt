n: From a Mandarin teacher's reel on how characters are built. 口 is a picture of an open mouth. Many characters with 口 in them have to do with the mouth. In everyday speech the mouth is 嘴 (Mandarin zuǐ, Cantonese 嘴 zeoi2).
nt: 口 həu55 "mouth" is in Stephen Li's dictionary.
