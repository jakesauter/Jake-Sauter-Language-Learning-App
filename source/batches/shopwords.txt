@ Shopping | Sell

y: 賣/maai6/0
m: 卖/mài/0
t: 卖/mai32/0
s: Vender/0
f: Vendre/0
d: Verkaufen/0
n: Memory trick: in simplified writing 卖 (sell) is 买 (buy) with 十 (ten) on top: the seller is the one who walks away with more money. In traditional writing it's 士 on top of 買.
nt: From Stephen Li's dictionary: 卖 mai32 “to sell”. Buy is mai33: only the tone changes, just like in Cantonese.
